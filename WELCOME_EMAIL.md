# Welcome email (ROADMAP.md item 275)

Paste into Buttondown → Settings → Subscribing → Welcome email.
Evergreen on purpose: Buttondown's welcome email is static, so no dates
and no seasonal links. Under 120 words.

**Subject:** You're in — here's what to expect

**Body:**

You're in. Every Thursday morning you'll get what's on this weekend in
Arlington Heights, Des Plaines, Mount Prospect, Palatine and Wheeling.

Can't wait until Thursday? Here's this weekend:
https://withintenmiles.com/this-weekend/?utm_source=email&utm_campaign=welcome

One small favor: add hello@withintenmiles.com to your contacts so
Thursday's email doesn't land in Promotions or Spam.

And a question: which town are you in, and what do you wish we
covered? Just reply. A real person reads every answer.

— Within Ten

---

Why these four parts, in this order: what arrives and when; one useful
next step; the deliverability ask (the list's only issue so far was
found in Spam, item 239); and the reply ask, which is both the strongest
inbox signal a new sender can get and how the site learns what readers
want.
