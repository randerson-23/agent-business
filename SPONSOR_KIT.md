## This is a recommendation, not an ad slot

Every region shows exactly one business at a time, framed as "Local
Recommendation" with a one-line reason why we picked them — not a banner
competing with ten other banners. That framing is backed by real numbers:
Nextdoor found that **79% of neighbors acted on a local recommendation**
they saw there, **81% of them within days**, and that local
recommendations consistently out-rank conventional business directories
and display ads for trust. A recommendation only works because it's
genuine, so the reason has to be real — a family that's used you, a
detail that makes you the obvious pick for this audience, anything true
and specific beats generic ad copy.

## Why sponsor

- **A recommendation, not an ad.** Framed as "we picked them," with your
  one-line reason shown to every reader — the format research consistently
  shows outperforms banner ads for trust and action.
- **Hyperlocal, opt-in audience.** Every reader is specifically looking at
  the region you sponsor. No wasted impressions.
- **Affluent, family-dense ZIPs.** Both 60056 (Mount Prospect) and 60005
  (Arlington Heights) run ~$100K median household income, well above the
  national median, with a strong skew toward married households with
  kids — a good match for family services, healthcare, home services, and
  local retail.
- **One business per region per week.** No banner-blindness, no competing
  ads on the page — just the one recommendation.
- **Simple, honest reach.** We publish weekly and report actual pageviews
  for your issue on request. No inflated "impressions."
