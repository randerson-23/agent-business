🏃 Trunk or Treat

Sat Oct 24, 10:00 AM

Arlington Heights

Join us for Trunk or Treat on Saturday, October 24th, at Pioneer Park from 10AM-12:30PM! Dress in your favorite costume to trick-or-treat at 40+ trunks and booths, enjoy inflatables and a giant slide, meet some Star Wars characters, and get a souvenir photo strip from the photo booth. Our Halloween DJ will pump up the party for the best Monster Bash around! Ages 2-13. Children must be registered and accompanied by an adult – $10 pre-registration through noon on Friday, 10/23. Walk-in registration at $15 at Pioneer Park only from 9am to 12pm on Saturday, 10/24. Adults do not need to register.
