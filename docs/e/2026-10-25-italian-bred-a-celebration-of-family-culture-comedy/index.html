Italian Bred, starring the incomparable Candice Guardino, is a vibrant, hilarious, and deeply relatable theatrical experience that has captivated audiences across the country. Drawing on her Italian-American roots, Guardino delivers a one-woman show that is both uproarious and heartwarming, blending sharp comedy, poignant storytelling, and a celebration of family and tradition. This performance transcends cultural boundaries, offering humor and insight that resonate with audiences from all walks of life, whether or not they share Italian heritage.
